Privacy Policy – Wobbly Wings

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Wobbly Wings ("the app") is a free arcade game published by Devansh Joshi ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos or any other personal information ourselves.

The app stores the following only on your device: your best score, your selected plane, and your sound on/off setting. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), ad interactions and diagnostic information. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. In regions where required (for example the EEA and UK) you will be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data security and retention

Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not operate any server that stores your personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
